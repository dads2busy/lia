Create a supply chain network for the ATmega328P microprocessor.
Determine its components, subcomponents and raw materials.
Here is a link to its datasheet: https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/ATmega48A-PA-88A-PA-168A-PA-328-P-DS-DS40002061A.pdf
For each component, define the approximate percentage of the component that is made from the raw material.
Identify the top 3 suppliers of each component, subcomponent, and raw material.
Identify any manufacturing equipment required to produce components or process the raw materials.
For each supplier, its market share of the associated component, the volume product (and a unit of the volume), the supplier's country, website address,  physical address, and a google maps link to the physical address.
Cite all of your references by identifying web links or textual references to the datasheet. Citations should be included in the node or edge metadata of the network.
The output should be JSON file in node-link format.
